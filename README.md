## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**LMMS** is an open-source audio sequencer for Windows, designed to provide a powerful and user-friendly interface for music production. As a free alternative to FL Studio, LMMS allows you to compose, arrange, and produce music directly on your computer without needing extensive musical knowledge. With its latest version, you can enjoy a comprehensive toolset tailored for both beginners and seasoned producers.

<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Independent Bass and Rhythm Editor** — Create and convert loops effortlessly.
